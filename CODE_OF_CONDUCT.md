# Code of Conduct

Jet Browser is built in the open for people working on browser runtimes, automation, and agent infrastructure. We want technical disagreement to stay useful, specific, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give reproducible context for bugs, benchmarks, and compatibility claims.
- Respect privacy, credentials, and the authorization boundaries of browser sessions.
- Accept corrections and make room for different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep issues, pull requests, and community spaces focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publication of private credentials or session data, deliberate disruption, and repeated bad-faith promotion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through [MASAKA support](https://masaka-ai.vercel.app/docs/support/). Do not include passwords, API keys, cookies, browser profiles, private tickets, or other secrets. Maintainers will limit access to reports to the people needed to review them.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
